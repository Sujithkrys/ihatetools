## Tips for Using Placeholder Text Effectively

- **Match the length to real content.** If your actual homepage headline will be 6-8 words, don't test your layout with a 20-word lorem ipsum headline, it'll give you a false sense of how the design handles real content.
- **Use paragraph mode for body copy, word mode for headlines and buttons.** Testing a button with three paragraphs of lorem ipsum doesn't tell you anything useful about button text wrapping.
- **Swap in real copy before final review.** Lorem ipsum is for structural testing, not for client presentations or a polished final product. Stakeholders tend to focus on the fact it's gibberish rather than the layout you're trying to show them.
- **Test with both short and long variants.** Generate a short version and a long version of the same block to see how your design breaks (or doesn't) at content length extremes.
- **Combine with your word counter.** If you need placeholder text that hits an exact word count target for a layout test, generate a batch and verify it with the [Word & Character Counter](/tools/word-counter).
